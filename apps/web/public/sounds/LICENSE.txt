Changes made for Distop
-----------------------
No unprocessed SND WAV is included in this directory. Each output combines at
least two SND01 sources, changes pitch and timing, applies new attack/release
envelopes, adds an original synthesized layer and echo, filters the result, and
is newly mastered to a different duration and peak level.
